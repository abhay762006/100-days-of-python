# Higher or Lower — Instagram Followers 🎯

A Python guessing game where the player compares two randomly selected personalities and guesses **who has more Instagram followers**.

## 🎮 How It Works

* Two random personalities are selected from `game_data`.
* Their name, description, and country are displayed.
* The player chooses who they think has the higher follower count.
* A correct answer increases the score.
* The player can choose to play again after each round.
